Status: descriptive research and three concept previews; **human selection pending**.

## Product basis

The new Arabic RTL app must give one visible entry to Huroof and EntaSa7, with one
account, catalog and wallet. Huroof remains a realtime team board game; EntaSa7
remains host-operated quiz play on a shared screen. The preview layouts show both
entry choices, one host action and one join action. They do not specify actual
questions, categories, balances, scores or playable content.

## Three alternatives for human selection

| ID | Working name | Mood and composition | Product fit and tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `INT-CONCEPT-A` | **لَمّة** | Warm gathering room; violet/coral/yellow tactile game surfaces; large friendly Arabic title | Makes family and group play approachable; requires strict restraint in dense admin and TV views |
| `INT-CONCEPT-B` | **ميدان** | High contrast black/white competition frame with one fuchsia action accent | Strong host/score visibility and TV legibility; needs careful warmth for study and account screens |
| `INT-CONCEPT-C` | **مِداد** | Quiet paper/ink/green editorial scene with spacious choices | Supports reading and study; live play needs a stronger phase, timer and score hierarchy |

The images are comparable desktop direction previews at 1586×992. They are not
screenshots of a functioning implementation. Generated Arabic microcopy may be
imperfect; production copy must be manually written and checked. A illustrates
the actual 25-hex board only as a static motif; B deliberately omits its full
board; C uses loose letter cards. None authorizes a rules or topology change.
