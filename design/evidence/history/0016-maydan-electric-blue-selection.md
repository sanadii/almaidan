## VISUAL CONTRACT — selected electric-blue replacement homepage

- **Product mood and visual concept:** A contemporary premium Arabic live-play studio. The electric blue field is confident and immersive; bright yellow, white and a contained lime game focal point make it playful without retro outlines or simulated 3D lettering.
- **Dominant focal point and scan order:** Lead with a large Arabic **ميدان** statement and the available Huroof invitation, then its verified-host and guest-join paths. A single original contained Huroof motif illustrates the game. Enta is a clearly secondary coming-soon invitation, not a competing equal card.
- **Information density:** Entry is 4/10. Keep ample blue negative space, short copy, one dominant hero and grouped actions. Remove the previous two thick bordered poster panels and redundant decorative stickers.
- **Desktop and mobile composition:** Desktop uses a spacious asymmetric RTL hero: headline/action column on the right and one contained board/game stage on the left, followed by a compact create/join dock and a smaller Enta teaser. Keep the first screen decisive. At 320px stack headline, Huroof stage, start/join controls and Enta teaser, with no clipped Arabic, horizontal scroll or targets under 44px. Preserve physical board axes.
- **Typography roles:** Use licensed Arabic-capable Lalezar for short display/game names and IBM Plex Sans Arabic for labels, controls and body. Draw headings as selectable DOM text with natural shaping and no joined-letter tracking. Room codes remain isolated LTR. Do not copy Honk's Latin font or the generated preview's 3D letter effects.
- **Palette and surfaces:** Lead with electric cobalt/sky blue, white and precise yellow highlights; reserve lime green for the contained Huroof motif and one primary available action. Darker tonal blue may define the game stage; avoid warm peach, purple and red as dominant colors. Prefer flat opaque/tonal surfaces, subtle dividers and little or no shadow. Set accessible contrast for every control and status.
- **Spacing and shape rules:** Use a coherent 8px rhythm, broad 48–64px desktop section separation, clean contemporary corners and careful alignment. A few original circles/speech cues can support the stage, but the composition should be restrained and premium. No thick black outlines, crowded rounded-card grid or random stickers.
- **Icon or imagery direction:** One original code-native Huroof 25-cell staggered board motif, presented as a static illustration rather than fake match ownership, plus a small original Enta shared-screen cue. Do not import the Honk phone, emoji, people, generated preview pixels or unlicensed assets.
- **Interaction and UI states:** Preserve real verified-host login/create route, guest room-code join and validation, account navigation, focus, loading/error/disabled reasons, and Enta's explicit unavailable state. Do not invent credits, playable Enta, users, live scores or content.
- **Motion restraint:** Fast finite interaction feedback only; no floating objects, bouncing type, looping particles or moving controls. Reduced motion stays static.
- **Explicit anti-patterns:** No warm-poster card structure, 90s-style heavy borders/shadows, 3D/neon lettering, gradient overload, generic dashboard tiles, copied Honk page or app screenshot, misleading live-match data, or styling other routes before human live homepage approval.

Implement only the combined homepage and entry shell in the isolated integration checkout, verify desktop/320px light/dark and functional/accessible behavior, then ask for an explicit human live review. This selection does not approve the rendered implementation or authorize visual expansion to games and supporting screens.
