## VISUAL CONTRACT — homepage correction within selected 0012 direction

- **Product mood and visual concept:** ميدان is a bright, playful Arabic type poster on calm peach. Adapt the reference's color roles and bold geometry to original game content, while retaining 0012's selected Warm poster direction.
- **Dominant focal point and scan order:** The oversized near-black ميدان wordmark leads, followed by a short choice prompt, then the available Huroof game and its real start/join actions, then the clearly unavailable Enta game. Account and status support those decisions.
- **Information density:** 4/10 on entry. The peach canvas and whitespace carry most of the view; neither game invitation becomes a full-frame saturated block.
- **Desktop and mobile composition:** Center the large Arabic title in an airy hero, with original bright burst/circle/capsule accents around its edges. Put game choices below in light paper fields; emphasize Huroof by scale and working controls, Enta by violet accent and explicit coming-soon state. At 320px stack title, Huroof, its controls, and Enta; keep 44px targets and no overflow.
- **Typography roles:** Keep licensed Lalezar for large, short Arabic display and game names and IBM Plex Sans Arabic for body, labels, buttons and status. Preserve joined shaping and LTR room-code isolation. Do not import the Latin source typeface or rasterize text.
- **Palette and surface treatment:** Use a pale peach canvas close to the reference, near-black type, light cream/white content surfaces, and saturated red-orange, lemon yellow, electric lime and bright violet as discrete stickers, callouts, key highlights and actions. Keep distinct contrast-safe action ink. Avoid the gold-dominant Huroof block, muted violet Enta block, olive-green dominance, heavy texture and dull color mixing seen in the rejected render. Actual Huroof crimson/emerald team semantics remain separate.
- **Spacing and shape rules:** Generous 24–48px group spacing, one large hero, light paper invitations, simple capsule labels and original irregular bursts. Bright shapes frame text without overlap; any game board motif remains a truthful 25-cell staggered hex identity.
- **Icon or imagery direction:** Code-drawn original circles, stars and speech/capsule shapes only. The exact Refero image, brand asset and stickers are research evidence and do not ship. No fake live game data.
- **Interaction and UI states:** Preserve true host verification, guest join, loading, error, disabled reason, keyboard focus, and Enta unavailable state. Color never carries status alone. Keep light/dark/system mode legible.
- **Motion restraint:** Only brief finite press/focus feedback; no ambient moving stickers. Honor reduced motion.
- **Explicit anti-patterns:** No giant filled gold/purple game panels, black/white/fuchsia return, copied Fictional logo/composition, low-contrast lime text, generic dashboard cards, decorative elements that hide actions, or playable Enta/credit claims before service and finance gates pass.

The 0012 three-concept evidence and human selection remain the chosen design family. This precise owner correction refines it rather than selecting a fourth concept. It authorizes a corrected homepage only.
