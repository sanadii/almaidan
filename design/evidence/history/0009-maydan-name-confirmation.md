After selection, a public name search found [“منصه ميدان / Maydan” on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/jo/app/%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B5%D9%87-%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%86/id6808385717), which describes Arabic group trivia, and [“Al Maydan” on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hassoud.almaydan), a quiz app. This is evidence of similar names in a nearby product category, not a trademark clearance or finding of legal rights.

The owner was shown those links and asked:

> Given the existing Arabic trivia apps named Maydan/Al Maydan, should the combined app keep the selected B visual style but use a different product name, or keep “ميدان” as the name? If changing it, please provide the new Arabic name.

The exact owner answer was:

> Keep ميدان as the name

The current authorized product name remains **ميدان**, and concept B remains the selected visual direction. Domain purchase, merchant identity, and any formal name clearance remain separate launch decisions. This record does not count as live homepage approval and does not authorize expanding the new visual system beyond the homepage and entry shell.
