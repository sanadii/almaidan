## Scoped visual contract — homepage and entry shell only

- **Mood and concept:** keep concept B's crisp Arabic competition stage as the dominant direction. Add friendly, editorial playfulness through typography and a few shapes. The references provide cues, not brand assets or a replacement palette.
- **Focal point and scan order:** headline and equal حروف/إنت صح choices first; create and join actions second; account, catalog and availability status third. Decoration cannot outrank an action or imply an unavailable feature is playable.
- **Density:** 4/10 on the public homepage. Keep grouped whitespace and one dominant focal point per viewport.
- **Desktop and mobile:** preserve a bounded RTL stage, two clear choices on desktop and stacked choices/actions at 320px. Treat any badge or bubble as a small accent that never overlays controls, game labels or status.
- **Typography:** IBM Plex Sans Arabic remains the production family. Use a bolder, larger 700-weight headline and confident game-name hierarchy, with short readable supporting copy. Do not track joined Arabic, distort letterforms or import the Latin typeface in the first reference.
- **Palette and surfaces:** retain near-black, white/soft-white and one strong fuchsia action accent from 0008. Do not import the references' blue, green, yellow, purple or orange as new semantic UI colors. Keep opaque surfaces and accessible contrast.
- **Spacing and shapes:** keep 0008's geometric frames and 4px spacing rhythm. Permit one or two small sticker/bubble silhouettes or lightly angled labels for editorial emphasis; they must be code-native, decorative, static by default and subordinate to the choice/action layout. No scattered confetti or rounded-card system.
- **Icons and imagery:** preserve code-rendered labelled game marks and exact Huroof board semantics. Do not copy either reference's wordmark, phone illustration, stickers, icon drawings or text. No fake match data.
- **Interaction and states:** preserve verified-host, guest join, unavailable Enta, loading/error, keyboard focus, 44px targets and answer/privacy rules. Decoration is hidden from accessibility; status remains real text.
- **Motion:** finite 90–120ms press and roughly 200ms state changes only; reduced motion is static. No bouncing stickers or ambient loops.
- **Anti-patterns:** no multicolor rebrand, Latin display-font substitution, copied composition, mock phone product shot, fake scores/credits, decorative controls or visual treatment of unavailable Enta as ready.

This is a refinement within the selected B family, not a new direction selection. It authorizes updating the existing combined homepage and entry shell for the owner's live review. It does not approve that live page or expand styling to gameplay, account, admin or commerce routes.
