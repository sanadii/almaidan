## Product brief

Design one Arabic RTL entry for **ميدان**, the future combined Huroof and EntaSa7 app. The first scan must make two games and their status clear, followed by host and join actions. Huroof uses a 25-cell staggered hex board; Enta is host operated on a shared screen. No invented balances, scores, categories, customer activity or available Enta match CTA. Generated previews are exploratory artwork; production UI must be accessible DOM with real states and content.

The owner rejected the implemented black/white/fuchsia homepage and directed attention to two supplied screenshots. Both have far more expressive type, saturated color fields and high-impact play shapes than the rejected page. A small angled badge on an otherwise unchanged grid does not meet that direction.

## Three preview briefs

1. **A — Warm poster play.** Primary source: Fictional Typeface. Peach/cream stage, very large confidently drawn Arabic **ميدان**, bold black forms, a controlled ring of original bright sticker bursts. Two distinct game choices should feel like poster invitations, with status and host/join sequence still legible. This is the most exuberant typographic option.
2. **B — Electric live stage.** Primary source: Honk. Electric-blue field, short yellow/white Arabic display line, one contained game motif drawn for ميدان instead of a phone mockup, generous blue negative space and a few original speech/burst shapes. The two games remain equally legible as choices, and the unavailable Enta state stays unmistakable. This is the strongest TV/live-play option.
3. **C — Paper-cut game studio.** Primary source: SuperHi. Pale blue/white canvas with large original paper-cut polygons clipped at the edges, centered high-confidence Arabic title and distinct game choices, rounded bold actions. This is a softer family/group-play option that still has a full visual system rather than a quiet card grid.

The name **ميدان** is fixed by the owner. All three previews will be comparable desktop homepage directions with actual bitmap identity, dimensions and hashes recorded in a separate exact-three manifest. Only the owner's later explicit choice will authorize a new visual contract and replacement homepage code. A subsequent human live review remains the gate for visual expansion.
