## Reference lock

**Primary:** Playful controls the spacious cream canvas, large confident central statement, narrow supporting copy, disciplined magenta action accent, and overlapping original tactile game artwork. The source uses an English italic headline; Arabic must reproduce confidence through appropriate letterforms and line breaks, never mechanical slant or negative tracking on joined script.

**Borrow only:** LottieFiles contributes clear control hierarchy, quiet navigation and polished original illustrative assets. Quizlet contributes cards that show understandable game previews rather than generic feature icons. Neither contributes a competing palette or whole-page composition.

**Other references:** bunny.net shows the value of a cohesive, high-quality illustration but its dark space scene is not selected. Busuu supports readable controls and accessible grouping; its full blue hero is not selected. Family demonstrates restrained chrome surrounding characters; its scattered mascot compositions are not selected.

**Reject:** averaging six palettes; a full blue page; retro stickers and thick outlines; repeated equal-weight boxed sections; copied mascots; giant heading with no artwork; fabricated screenshots, testimonials, category counts or availability; continuous decorative motion around gameplay or input fields.
