## How to review

Fill the pale yellow columns **G–M** on the `Review` sheet. Leave the source columns unchanged. You may sort or filter the rows, but keep every `Source ID` and both hash columns with its question. Return the five `.xlsx` files with their filenames unchanged.

- **Review decision:** `Approve` means the original question, answer and source difficulty are accepted. `Revise` means enter the corrected wording or answer and the final difficulty. `Hold` means more evidence or work is needed. `Reject` means do not use the row. `Already live` confirms an existing duplicate.
- **Final tier:** use `easy`, `medium` or `hard` when revising difficulty. The corresponding question values are 200, 400 and 600. Source tiers are provisional until reviewed.
- **Media ready:** for image/audio/video questions, enter `Yes` only after the exact playable media and rights, visual identity and delivery have been reviewed. Blank or `No` leaves the media hold in place.
- **Review notes and reviewer:** record the reason for changes, holds or rejections and who reviewed the row. An empty decision remains unreviewed.

The `Audit finding` column is a starting point, not a final decision: 14,715 rows need content review; 190 need media preparation; 49 are local runtime candidates; 22 exactly match live questions; 3 need semantic duplicate review; and 4 have ambiguous prompts. All six groups are included so every source ID can be reconciled on return. For a quick start, the four explicitly ambiguous rows are in packet 1 at worksheet rows **1983** and **2106**, and packet 3 at rows **490** and **655**.
