## Music and video decision

**No downloadable audio or video clips are requested by these three primary exports.** Topup has 140 media-free music trivia rows with a recording-association verification hold: confirm the artist/recording from suitable credits, then perform ordinary editorial review. An artist/catalog page does not grant a clip for the game. Clean70's other recovered/master rows and live-humming concepts are not new media acquisition requests in this T37 continuation. Partial201 supplies text trivia only.

If a later source-backed question actually needs a song excerpt or video, open a separate clip batch. Record the exact recording/performance, rightsholder authorization or clearly applicable license, source file identity, permitted use, exact start/end, duration, derivative hash and playback rules. Do not extract a stream merely because it can be viewed or embedded; [YouTube's terms](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) distinguish permitted viewing/embedding from downloading and reuse. No future clip is counted as acquired in this plan.
